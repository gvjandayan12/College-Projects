**Introudction**
- Purpose
  
  A database system is designed to efficiently store, organize, retrieve, and manage data, providing a structured way to handle large volumes of information. It solves the problem of data redundancy,         inconsistency, and difficulty in accessing or updating information by centralizing data management. This allows for secure, scalable, and reliable operations across various applications. The primary users     and stakeholders include database administrators (DBAs), who manage and maintain the database; developers, who use it to build applications; and end-users, who access data for decision-making or daily         operations. Businesses, governments, and organizations rely on database systems to ensure seamless data management and support for critical processes.

  The primary users are people who are interested in buying clothes and pants online, it solves the problem of users not being able to buy items physically due to unavailability.

**System Requirements**
- **Functional Requirements**

    - CRUD
- **User Authentication**

    - Allow users to register, log in, and access their personalized accounts.
 
  **Product Management**

  - Admins can add, update, or remove products from the catalog.
  - Users can browse products by categories such as clothing and shoes.

  **Order Management**

  - Users can place orders, and admins can manage and process these orders.
  - The system will maintain records of all purchases for reporting and tracking.
